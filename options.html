Keyboard shortcut

Copies the BibTeX for the selected DOI, or for the page you are on.

Needs Ctrl or Alt plus a key (e.g. Alt+Shift+B). Avoid Ctrl+B: it toggles the bookmarks sidebar in Firefox-based browsers and is "bold" in Overleaf and Google Docs. You can also change it under Add-ons → gear icon → Manage Extension Shortcuts.
